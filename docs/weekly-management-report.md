# Weekly Management Report

This report is designed for leadership review.

It intentionally avoids billable vs non-billable language and instead highlights:

- exceptions
- non-compliance
- efficiency
- long-working clients split by article time and manager time for meeting prioritization

## 5. Long-Working Clients

This is the key meeting-priority section.

The idea is to split client time into:

- article time
- manager time
- total client time

This helps management decide:

- which clients need partner attention
- which clients need process correction
- which clients need resource rebalancing

### Suggested table

| Client | Article Time | Manager Time | Total Time | Trend | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| `<client>` | `<number>` | `<number>` | `<number>` | Up / Flat / Down | High / Medium / Low |

### Priority rules

Mark a client as `High` priority if any of these apply:

- total hours are consistently high
- manager time is unusually high
- article time is unusually high and needs review
- work is increasing week over week
- the client has repeated exceptions or rework

### Management interpretation

- High article time: likely execution-heavy client, may need process support
- High manager time: likely supervision-heavy client, may need tighter planning or delegation
- High combined time: should be placed on the weekly meeting agenda

## 8. Recommended Tone For Management

Keep the language factual and concise:

- say `exception` instead of `problem`
- say `non-compliance` instead of `delay`
- say `high load` instead of `overworked`
- say `priority review` instead of `discussion`

Avoid:

- billable vs non-billable
- detailed operational chatter
- staff-level blame language
